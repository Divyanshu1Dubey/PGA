India-Russia Youth Dialogue 2025

Online Event | National Youth Day India

We are thrilled to announce India-Russia Youth Dialogue 2025 (Online)! This initiative is organized by the National Preparatory Committee India for the Directorate of World Youth Festival, Russia.

About the Event

Join us as we celebrate the legacy of Swami Vivekananda, a global youth icon, and promote collaboration between Indian and Russian youth to address pressing global challenges. This landmark dialogue brings together 1,000 participants (500 from each country) to engage in meaningful conversations about sustainable futures and digital diplomacy.

Event Details

  • Date: 12th January, 2025
  • Time: 5:30 PM (IST) Onwards
  • Format: Online
  • Occasion: National Youth Day India, commemorating the Birth Anniversary of Swami Vivekananda
  • Participants: 1,000 participants (500 from each country)
  • Certificates: E-Certificates for all attendees

Key Themes

Sustainable Futures

Youth-Driven Solutions for SDGs - exploring how young leaders can contribute to achieving the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.

Digital Diplomacy

Youth Leadership in the Age of Technology - understanding the role of digital platforms in fostering international cooperation and diplomacy.

Event Highlights

  • 1,000 participants (500 from each country)
  • E-Certificates for all attendees
  • Inspirational talks to empower youth action
  • Interactive sessions on global challenges

Legacy of Swami Vivekananda

This event commemorates the Birth Anniversary of Swami Vivekananda, one of India's most influential spiritual and social leaders. His message of universal brotherhood, youth empowerment, and service to humanity continues to inspire millions around the world. The India-Russia Youth Dialogue embodies his vision of connecting cultures and fostering global understanding through youth engagement.

The dialogue serves as a platform where young minds from both nations can exchange ideas, build lasting connections, and work together towards creating sustainable solutions for global challenges.
